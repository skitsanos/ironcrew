# IC-009 — Crew effectiveness evidence is too small for product claims

## Required outcome and acceptance

The current live-provider receipt is a useful smoke: three synthetic cases,
one GPT-4.1 repetition, and exact deterministic scoring show better evidence
completeness for DAG/collaborative crews at substantially higher token and
latency cost. It cannot establish statistical reliability, model generality,
domain transfer, or return on cost. Contract mode is oracle-backed and is not
effectiveness evidence.

Required outcome:

- run the complete six-case corpus with predeclared thresholds and enough
  repetitions to report uncertainty, paired wins/ties/losses, cost, and latency;
- add at least two representative domain packs derived from real intended use,
  with versioned inputs and deterministic or independently reviewed oracles;
- compare single-call, deterministic DAG, and collaborative variants under the
  same model/settings and randomized order;
- record failures, parse/schema validity, token/cached-token usage, latency,
  revision, binary/dataset hashes, and provider/model identity; and
- define when the simpler workflow wins so the benchmark guides topology choice
  rather than merely marketing crews.

Acceptance is an honest reproducible report, not a requirement that every crew
variant win. Provider cost and rate limits must remain bounded before execution.

## Progress checkpoint

On 2026-08-08, the evaluator gained a strict, versioned decision plan before
another paid run. The reviewed five-repetition plan covers all six cases and
admits exactly 90 CLI runs, 240 planned provider calls, 147,000 requested
maximum output tokens, 30 matched pairs per topology, and six unique cases. It
pins the exact Lua flow hash and canonical per-variant call/token accounting;
changed flow content, dishonest accounting, underpowered samples, oversized
inputs, empty model identity, or missing live provider/repetition declarations
fail before the binary or provider is started.

Report v2 records the plan/provider/model identities, unknown failed-run usage
as `null`, resource-measurement coverage, paired case-cluster uncertainty, and
a Bonferroni-adjusted 95% family-wise decision across the two crew comparisons.
The predeclared result can be `insufficient_evidence`, `single_preferred`, or
`crew_qualified`; contract mode is schema-fenced to `not_applicable`. The
evaluator unit suite passed 29/29, and the offline contract passed 18 CLI runs,
48 mock calls, and 36/36 grounded fixture answers. No live or paid provider
call was made in this checkpoint.

IC-009 remains in progress until the approved full live receipt and at least
two representative, independently reviewed domain packs establish reliability
beyond the synthetic corpus and one provider/model configuration.
