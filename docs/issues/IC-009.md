# IC-009 — Crew effectiveness evidence is too small for product claims

## Required outcome and acceptance

The historical first live-provider receipt is a useful smoke: three synthetic
cases, one GPT-4.1 repetition, and exact deterministic scoring show better
evidence completeness for DAG/collaborative crews at substantially higher
token and latency cost. It cannot establish statistical reliability, model
generality, domain transfer, or return on cost. Contract mode is oracle-backed
and is not effectiveness evidence.

Required outcome:

- run the complete six-case corpus with predeclared thresholds and enough
  repetitions to report uncertainty, paired wins/ties/losses, cost, and latency;
- add at least two representative domain packs derived from real intended use,
  with versioned inputs and deterministic or independently reviewed oracles;
- compare single-call, deterministic DAG, and collaborative variants under the
  same model/settings and randomized order;
- record failures, parse/schema validity, token/cached-token usage, latency,
  revision, binary/dataset hashes, and provider/model identity; and
- define when the simpler workflow wins so the benchmark guides topology choice
  rather than merely marketing crews.

Acceptance is an honest reproducible report, not a requirement that every crew
variant win. Provider cost and rate limits must remain bounded before execution.

## Progress checkpoint

On 2026-08-08, the evaluator gained a strict, versioned decision plan before
another paid run. The reviewed five-repetition plan covers all six cases and
admits exactly 90 CLI runs, 240 planned provider calls, 147,000 requested
maximum output tokens, 30 matched pairs per topology, and six unique cases. It
pins the exact Lua flow hash and canonical per-variant call/token accounting;
changed flow content, dishonest accounting, underpowered samples, oversized
inputs, empty model identity, or missing live provider/repetition declarations
fail before the binary or provider is started.

Report v2 records the plan/provider/model identities, unknown failed-run usage
as `null`, resource-measurement coverage, paired case-cluster uncertainty, and
a Bonferroni-adjusted 95% family-wise decision across the two crew comparisons.
The predeclared result can be `insufficient_evidence`, `single_preferred`, or
`crew_qualified`; contract mode is schema-fenced to `not_applicable`. The
evaluator unit suite passed 29/29, and the offline contract passed 18 CLI runs,
48 mock calls, and 36/36 grounded fixture answers. No live or paid provider
call was made in this checkpoint.

On 2026-08-11, active OpenAI defaults, generated scaffolds, current demos, and
the planned evaluator model moved from the GPT-4.1 family to

## Outcome and validation

**Resolved locally on 2026-08-12.**

On 2026-08-12, the predeclared GPT-5.6 Luna plan completed all 180/180 local
live-provider runs: 12 cases, five repetitions, and 60 matched pairs for each
of the DAG and collaborative comparisons against the single-call baseline.
All 180 runs executed, parsed, and matched the output schema; token, latency,
and cost-estimate coverage was complete. The release binary and source
manifest stayed unchanged throughout the run. The frozen aggregate corpus,
plan, flow, and release-binary SHA-256 values were respectively:

- `bb73ad0d4835a407e22bc35de1562a9f600e33583ec219e40eba2b7b4b0c45cf`
- `41367a71086a3ed51cea426c6936dfa9e4f75e62e4f8130b5d69801dd6c62d54`
- `bbbdc021f3be7befbacbe4a5f96b6b9994f39b6f8d96e935ab507bd65ea1bfe8`
- `d148eca7e2166bf0e8290b8b438b972452d74d9b7766cdaae8d136d522b217a8`

The single-call baseline achieved 0.6500 mean grounded correctness. The DAG
achieved 0.7833, a +0.1333 paired mean delta with a Bonferroni-adjusted 97.5%
interval of [0.0500, 0.2167], 18 wins, 40 ties, and 2 losses. Its 3.2113x mean
token and 4.5645x mean latency multipliers both remained inside the
predeclared 6x ceilings, so it met every selection check. The collaborative
variant improved mean grounded correctness to 0.7333 but did not qualify: its
interval crossed zero and its 6.7050x latency multiplier exceeded the 6x
ceiling. Therefore `crew_qualified` recommends the DAG for this exact bounded
configuration; it does not mean that every crew topology qualified or that
crews are generally superior.

Provider-reported usage totaled 289,816 prompt, 107,821 completion, and 397,637
total tokens, with zero cached tokens reported. Applying the frozen pricing
contract conservatively produced a $0.2018392 observed estimated upper bound,
well below the $2.7528 planned bound and $3 approval budget. This is a
token-derived estimate, not a provider invoice.
