# IC-011 — Legacy oversized Rust modules lack a growth ratchet

## Required outcome and acceptance

IronCrew's preferred 300-line target and 400-line ceiling cannot be enforced
honestly as a universal gate today. The 2026-08-07 inventory has 95 production
Rust modules: 50 exceed 300 lines, 39 exceed 400, and 12 exceed 1,000. The
largest, `src/engine/postgres_store.rs`, is 7,883 lines. A blanket IronFlow-style
limit would create an exception wall rather than improve design.

Required outcome:

- add a deterministic baseline policy that forbids new oversized modules and
  growth of legacy files without an explicit reviewed ceiling and rationale;
- fail on stale paths, unexplained growth, symlinks, and newly untracked exceptions;
- print the largest modules and responsibility-boundary guidance on every run;
- cap or ratchet the total exception budget downward rather than allowing it to grow;
- add standard-library-only checker tests and CI coverage; and
- prioritize cohesive extraction from PostgreSQL storage, SQLite storage, API
  handlers/conversations, run history, input bridge, and event infrastructure.

LOC is a review trigger, not a design score. Acceptance must reward real
responsibility boundaries and must not force mechanical one-function files.

## Reviewed temporary ceiling — 2026-08-11

The IC-007 through IC-020 replica, conversation, and storage remediation landed
before this issue's deterministic ratchet existed. Review accepted one temporary
ceiling at the resulting current sizes: `src/engine/postgres_store.rs` 8,892
lines, `src/engine/sqlite_store.rs` 3,840 lines, and
`src/api/conversations.rs` 2,423 lines. The change also extracted new lifecycle,
deployment, SSE, resource-metric, conversation-definition, JSON-boundary,
provider-policy, and request-image responsibilities into focused production
modules no larger than 300 lines.

This is a documented ceiling, not a resolution or permission for continued
growth. IC-011 remains open: its checker must ratchet from these values or lower,
reject further unexplained growth, and prioritize cohesive storage and
conversation-handler extraction. A last-minute mechanical split after the
PostgreSQL, two-process, Railway, and OpenShift acceptance runs would have
invalidated the reviewed source and platform evidence without improving the
responsibility boundaries.
