# IC-053 — Locked TLS dependencies fail the current security advisory gate

## Finding and evidence

The mandatory `cargo audit --deny warnings` gate during IC-052 verification on
2026-10-21 fails on six advisories in three locked transitive dependencies:

| Locked crate | Advisory IDs | Compatible patched version |
|---|---|---|
| `rustls 0.23.45` | RUSTSEC-2026-0346, -0347, -0348, -0349 | `0.23.46` or later |
| `rustls-webpki 0.103.15` | RUSTSEC-2026-0382 | `0.103.16` or later in the stable series |
| `webpki-root-certs 1.0.9` | RUSTSEC-2026-0383 | `1.0.10` or later |

The freshly fetched RustSec database contains 1,348 advisories; the lockfile has
415 dependencies. `cargo tree` traces Rustls/WebPKI through Reqwest and its TLS
stack; root certificates also appear in target-specific dependency edges.
The audit covers the complete lockfile. This is not a claim that all six
conditions are exploitable on the current host or with the enabled features.

The Rustls advisories concern session-value Debug output, rejected ECH buffered
data, certificate decompression allocation and key-exchange downgrade handling.
The WebPKI/root-certificate advisories concern distrusted intermediates. Primary
advisories are at `https://rustsec.org/advisories/<ID>`; baseline scanner evidence
is retained in ignored `target/ic-052-verification/audit-before-tls.log`.

IC-013 resolved an earlier unrelated transitive advisory and removed an audit
suppression. No existing issue covers these six newly observed locked versions.

## Required outcome and acceptance

Use compatible patched transitive versions without widening direct dependency
constraints or suppressing advisories. Review the exact lockfile delta, rerun
the denied-warning audit, and repeat Rust format/Clippy/all-target gates plus
the IC-052 mock HTTP, Lua and downstream Crew contracts on the final graph.
Preserve existing runtime settings and services. No release, push or deployment
is included in this local dependency repair.

## Outcome and validation

The final lockfile selects `rustls 0.23.46`, `rustls-webpki 0.103.16` and
`webpki-root-certs 1.0.10`. These are compatible transitive patch upgrades;
`Cargo.toml`, direct constraints and audit policy are unchanged. Resolver churn
on unrelated existing `syn`/`getrandom` edges was excluded after checking their
declared ranges, and `cargo metadata --locked` validates the resulting graph.
The lockfile diff contains only those three versions and checksums. Its SHA-256
is `c87e961faa4ccbe379900c4146c8c3c7f2d81ae60e81c09080e975979ccfaf8b`.

Resolved on 2026-10-21. The freshly fetched `cargo audit --deny warnings` passes
with no findings or suppressions (1,348 advisories, 415 locked packages).
`cargo metadata --locked`, Rust format, denied-warning all-target Clippy and
`cargo test --all-targets` all pass on the final graph: 1,557 tests, no failures,
three intentional ignores. IC-052 records the complete mock HTTP/cancellation,
Lua, downstream Crew and real-Arango App persistence receipts on that same
rebuilt runtime. Python/module-size/Bun-policy and issue-registry checks pass.

Only the three named version/checksum entries changed in `Cargo.lock`;
existing unrelated dependency edges and `Cargo.toml` are byte-identical to
HEAD. The final eight-file qualified source manifest remained unchanged during
the checks. Logs and fingerprints are under ignored
`target/ic-052-verification/`; the earlier failing audit remains historical
evidence at `audit-before-tls.log`. Both diffs and generated indexes pass checks.

This repairs the local locked graph and security gate. It does not claim a
release, hosted CI run, cross-platform execution or deployed image update.
