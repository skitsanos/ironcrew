# IC-008 — Live conversation control remains process-owned

## Required outcome and acceptance

PostgreSQL shares persisted conversation transcripts, but the live Lua
conversation handle, active turn, abort state, and conversation SSE channel
remain in one process. A message routed to another replica cannot safely resume
or broker the turn, so generic multi-replica deployments still require owner
routing or one replica for conversation workloads.

Required outcome:

- define fenced ownership for one active turn and deterministic behavior when
  flow source/model configuration changed since the persisted transcript;
- either broker commands to the owner or rehydrate a conversation safely from
  durable state after owner loss;
- make duplicate message admission and deletion races deterministic;
- provide bounded durable conversation SSE replay or a truthful unsupported boundary;
- preserve transcript byte/history limits and provider/tool cancellation; and
- pass start, message, history, delete, reconnect, owner-death, and rolling-restart
  cases through either of two real replicas.

Execution checkpointing for arbitrary runs is outside this issue unless the
conversation design explicitly requires and specifies it.
