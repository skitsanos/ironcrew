# IC-018 — Replica soak lacks retention-boundary steady-state evidence

## Outcome and validation

**Resolved locally on 2026-08-11.** The earlier 150-second provider-free soak
completed 253 runs but did not cross journal retention or cover representative
provider/tool, large-result, and conversation workloads. IC-018 now has a
canonical predeclared contract, a 30-minute direct two-process PostgreSQL
receipt, and separately bounded loopback-mock profiles.

The canonical declaration fixed the complete journal configuration and all
ceilings before admission. Two release processes completed 2,613/2,613
provider-free keyed runs over 1,800.651 workload seconds with zero readiness,
liveness, or workload failures and no deadlocks. Sixty-one observations crossed
the 600-second retention boundary; 40 were post-boundary, and the final
20-interval tail covered 602.081 seconds with 7,322 latency samples and no
ceiling violation.

Maximum retained journal state was 3,696 rows and 3,784,704 bytes against
ceilings of 8,192 and 8 MiB. Expired physical rows peaked at 5 against 128;
post-prune retained growth was zero rows/bytes against 512/1 MiB. Retention
state advanced in 41 intervals and PostgreSQL delete statistics in 40, while
the final run-event table reported 7,103 tuple deletions, 9 autovacuum runs,
29 autoanalyze runs, and 165 estimated dead rows. The delete deltas are
correlated aggregate prefix evidence, not per-batch causality. Prefix relation
size peaked at 11,984,896 bytes; WAL was 69,072,576 bytes; per-replica RSS
peaked at 17,924,096 and 17,989,632 bytes; and tail RSS growth was 16,384/0
bytes.

Delayed replay returned the required `409 cursor_expired`, one explicit
`retention` gap, and an unnumbered durable-record terminal with
`journal_complete: false`; the anchor had zero retained/physical rows and
recorded the retention boundary. Both processes exited 0 without forced kills,
runtime streams passed secret-canary scans without retaining raw logs, and
exact-prefix cleanup verified zero remaining database objects.

The supplemental direct-process receipt separately passed one provider/tool
profile with exactly two mock-provider calls and one counted tool effect, one
exact 65,536-byte result profile, and one two-turn warm-owner/cold-peer
committed conversation profile. Conversation revisions advanced `1 -> 2 -> 3`,
history matched revision 3, and PostgreSQL conversation SSE returned the
documented `409`. The receipt explicitly records that in-flight takeover was
not proven. Planned and actual paid-provider calls and cost were zero.

This resolves the reproducible provider-free local-process evidence gap. It
does not turn host RSS into a pod cgroup limit, bound non-journal records with a
24-hour lifetime, prove in-flight execution takeover or arbitrary exactly-once
effects, or provide live-provider, Railway, OpenShift, autoscaling, OOM, or
production-monitoring evidence. The enforced 128 MiB RSS ceiling used host
samples; the separate 1 GiB platform-memory comparator was not enforced and was
not the pass ceiling. The dirty source and binary remain unpublished.
