## Honest interpretation

This run is positive evidence for using a crew when evidence completeness is
valuable: the DAG and collaborative variants improved grounded correctness and
citation recall on every paired case or tied the single call. It is also clear
evidence of cost: compared with `single`, the DAG used about 4.7 times the total
tokens and 4.2 times the median latency, while the collaborative variant used
about 7.4 times the total tokens and 9.2 times the median latency.

It is not broad evidence that crews generally outperform simpler workflows.
Three synthetic cases and one repetition cannot establish statistical
reliability, model generality, or production return on cost. The next evidence
gate is the full six-case corpus with repeated runs, followed by representative
domain packs and predeclared decision thresholds. The earlier free-text smoke
report was rejected rather than promoted because its scorer allowed ambiguous
answer matching; only the exact-option-ID run is evidence here.
